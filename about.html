Our Story

We don't build booths. We build lasting impressions.

MEDAR BOOTHS is a full-service exhibition design & build studio headquartered in Dubai. From the first sketch to the final light fixture, we own every detail.

Our in-house team blends architecture, fabrication, and digital experience to deliver booths that don't just hold attention — they command the room.

— Journey Milestones

Twelve years in the making.

2013

The Studio Is Born

MEDAR BOOTHS opens its doors in Al Quoz with a three-person team and a single workshop.

2016

First 100 Booths

We deliver our 100th booth at GITEX, cementing our reputation across the Gulf region.

2019

Going International

Our first projects outside the GCC — Riyadh, Cairo, and Doha become regular fixtures.

2022

In-House Fabrication

We open a 4,000 sqm production facility, bringing every step of the build under one roof.

2024

Digital & Interactive

Full AV and interactive division launched — LED walls, holograms, and touch experiences.

2025

450 Booths & Counting

We cross 450 delivered booths across 28 countries — and we're just getting started.

— Process How we work

From first sketch to show floor.

01

Design

Concept, 3D visualization, and brand integration from day one. We sketch, iterate, and render until the vision is undeniable.

02

Fabrication

In-house production with premium materials and precision. CNC routing, joinery, metalwork, and finish — all under our roof.

03

Installation

On-site build, AV integration, and show-time support. Our crew works through the night so the booth is flawless by opening.
